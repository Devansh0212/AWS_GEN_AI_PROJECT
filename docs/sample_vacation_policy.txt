====================================================
ENTERPRISE HR POLICY: VACATION AND PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
====================================================
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applies To: All Full-Time Employees

1. VACATION ELECTION AND ACCRUAL
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year.
Vacation time accrues at a rate of 1.67 days per full month worked.

2. CARRY-OVER POLICY
Employees may carry over up to 5 unused vacation days into the following calendar year.
Any additional unused days beyond 5 days are forfeited on December 31st.

3. CONTRACTOR POLICY
Contractors and temporary workers are not eligible for paid vacation days.
Time off for contractors must be requested 2 weeks in advance and is unpaid.

4. REQUEST PROCEDURE
All vacation requests exceeding 3 consecutive days must be submitted via the HR portal
at least 10 business days prior to the planned leave start date.
